CDAO 2027: where industry meets mathematics and AI

The CASPAM Digital Artifact Olympiad invites students to build a working app, dashboard, simulator or decision tool on a mathematical model, and to show evidence that it helps.

Final: Thursday, 15 April 2027, Bahauddin Zakariya University, Multan

What is a digital artifact?

Not a report, but something people can use: a reorder tool for distributors, a timetable optimiser, a demand forecast for retailers or an advisory app for farmers. Independent ideas are welcome, and a verified customer is optional.

Choose your stream

One Olympiad, three distinct contributions. Select the stream that best describes the project’s main contribution.

Industrial

Working tools for operational and business decisions: analytics, optimization and industrial intelligence.

Show workflow fit and credible value. Customer, sales, HR, finance, supply chain, inventory, production, quality and service problems all count.

AI Research

Develop or evaluate AI methods: algorithms, benchmarking, explainability and computational intelligence.

Compare against baselines, document limitations and support reproducibility.

Mathematical

Tools for mathematical discovery, computation, modelling, visualization or learning.

Discovery, modelling, symbolic computation and interactive learning.

Rules and judging

Every entry connects an idea or problem, a mathematical or computational model, a digital artifact and credible evidence.

The essentials

  • Open to anyone: students of any institution, graduates and professionals. One team per person.
  • Teams of up to 3, including any supervisor or mentor.
  • PKR 2,500 per participant, paid by bank transfer or Raast.
  • Independent ideas are welcome without a customer.
  • Honest impact: state the indicator, baseline and target, and label results Measured if piloted or Projected with a measurement plan.
  • Observers welcome (registration fee PKR 2,500).

Judging rubric, 100 points

Problem exploration and validation evidence20 Clarity and importance of the problem15 Mathematical model and reasoning20 Functionality and usability of the artifact20 Measurable or credibly projected value15 Pitch and communication10

At least 2 independent judges score each entry.

Key dates

Registration opening, registration and fee deadline, Idea or Problem Profile, final submission and finalists announcement: to be announced. Final pitches, results and awards: Thursday, 15 April 2027. Deadlines close at 23:59 Pakistan time.
