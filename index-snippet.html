CDAO 2027

The CASPAM Digital Artifact Olympiad takes place at BZU Multan on Thursday, 15 April 2027. Teams build a working digital artifact on a mathematical model, in one of three streams:

  • Industrial: tools for operational and business decisions.
  • AI Research: developing or evaluating AI methods with baselines and reproducibility.
  • Mathematical: tools for discovery, computation, modelling, visualization or learning.
